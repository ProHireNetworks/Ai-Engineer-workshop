# Cultural Etiquette, Safety & Essential Travel Tips: India

## Cultural Etiquette & Sacred Sites
- **Footwear at Places of Worship:** Always remove footwear before entering Hindu temples, Sikh gurdwaras, Jain shrines, and mosques. Some temples also require heads to be covered (scarves/bandanas).
- **Attire Guidelines:** Modest dress covering shoulders and knees is appreciated in rural villages, historical religious complexes, and traditional markets.
- **Greetings:** The traditional greeting *Namaste* with palms pressed together at chest level is universal and deeply respectful across all regions.

## Financial & Digital Logistics
- **UPI Payments:** Unified Payments Interface (UPI) via PhonePe, Google Pay, and Paytm is accepted everywhere from five-star resorts to street-side coconut vendors. International travelers can obtain UPI One World at major airports.
- **Currency:** Indian Rupee (INR - ₹). Carry moderate cash (₹100 and ₹500 denominations) when venturing into remote mountain valleys or rural reserves.
- **Tipping Norms:** In dine-in restaurants, a 7% to 10% gratuity is standard unless a service charge is already included in the bill.

## Health, Water & Culinary Safety
- **Drinking Water:** Drink only sealed bottled water from reputed brands or filtered RO (reverse osmosis) water provided by reputable hotels. Avoid tap water and untreated ice cubes.
- **Street Food Caution:** Look for stalls with high local turnover, freshly cooked piping-hot items, and bottled water preparations.

## Connectivity & Transportation
- **SIM Cards:** Tourist eSIMs (Airtel or Jio) can be purchased at arrival international airports using passport copies and visa verification.
- **Rail Travel:** IRCTC operates the world's 4th largest railway network. Vande Bharat Express trains provide high-speed, world-class connectivity between major state capitals.
