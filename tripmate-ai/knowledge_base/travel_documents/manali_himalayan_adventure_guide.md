# Comprehensive Mountain & Adventure Guide: Manali, Himachal Pradesh

## Destination Overview
Perched at an altitude of 2,050 meters (6,726 feet) in the Beas River Valley, Manali is a premier Himalayan hill station offering high-altitude adventure sports, pine forests, apple orchards, and ancient wooden temples.

## Key Geographical Zones & Attractions

### Old Manali & Hadimba Temple
- **Hadimba Devi Temple:** A 16th-century four-tiered pagoda-style cedar wood temple nestled inside a centuries-old Dhungri deodar forest.
- **Old Manali Village:** Quaint wooden stone cottages, hippie cafes, live acoustic music, and riverside hiking trails leading to Jogini Waterfalls.

### Solang Valley & Atal Tunnel
- **Solang Valley:** Adventure playground for paragliding, zorbing, quad biking in summer, and amateur skiing during winter months.
- **Atal Tunnel & Sissu:** A 9.02 km engineering marvel under the Rohtang Pass connecting Kullu Valley to the high-altitude trans-Himalayan desert of Lahaul and Spiti. Sissu features turquoise glacier waterfalls and poplar trees.

### Rohtang Pass
- **High Altitude Pass:** Sitting at 3,978 meters (13,050 feet), Rohtang offers panoramic vistas of the Pir Panjal mountain range and perpetual snowbanks. Requires an advance environmental permit for vehicular entry.

## Adventure Activities & Trekking
- **Trekking Circuits:** Hampta Pass trek (crossover into Spiti), Bhrigu Lake alpine meadow trek, and Beas Kund glacier trek.
- **River Rafting:** Class II and III white-water rafting along the Beas River near Pirdi and Raison.

## Seasonality & Packing Advisory
- **Summer (April to June):** Perfect daytime temperatures (12°C to 25°C) ideal for trekking and escape from plains heat.
- **Winter (December to February):** Frequent heavy snowfall (-5°C to 8°C); heavy woolens, thermal innerwear, and waterproof snow boots are essential.
- **Transit:** Accessible via Bhuntar Airport (Kullu) or scenic overnight Volvo coaches from New Delhi / Chandigarh via the Kiratpur-Manali highway.
