# Comprehensive Travel Guide: Kerala (God's Own Country)

## Destination Overview
Located along the tropical Malabar Coast of southern India, Kerala is celebrated for its palm-fringed backwaters, misty Western Ghats hill stations, authentic Ayurvedic wellness traditions, and rich cultural biodiversity.

## Primary Regional Destinations

### Alleppey (Alappuzha) & Kumarakom Backwaters
- **Houseboat Cruises:** Traditional *Kettuvallams* crafted from wooden planks tied with coir ropes, converted into luxurious floating hotels traversing Lake Vembanad and quiet canal networks.
- **Village Canals:** Exploring Kuttanad by narrow wooden canoe to observe duck farming, paddy fields below sea level, and toddy tapping.

### Munnar (Western Ghats Tea Estates)
- **Top Attractions:** Eravikulam National Park (home to the endangered Nilgiri Tahr and purple Neelakurinji blossoms), Mattupetty Dam, and Anamudi Peak (highest peak in South India).
- **Tea Plantations:** Visiting century-old Kolukkumalai estate (world's highest tea plantation) for orthodox tea processing demonstrations.

### Fort Kochi (Colonial Maritime Heritage)
- **Landmarks:** Historic Chinese Fishing Nets (*Cheena Vala*), St. Francis Church (where Vasco da Gama was initially buried), Mattancherry Dutch Palace, and Jew Town antique boutiques.

## Ayurvedic Wellness & Culinary Traditions
- **Ayurvedic Rejuvenation:** Certified Panchakarma treatments, herbal oil massages (*Abhyanga*), and steam therapies using indigenous medicinal herbs.
- **Kerala Sadya:** Elaborate vegetarian feast served on banana leaves during festivals and traditional lunches, featuring Avial, Sambar, Thoran, Payasam, and red rice.
- **Seafood & Malabar Delicacies:** Karimeen Pollichathu (Pearl spot fish marinated in spicy paste and baked in banana leaf), appam with vegetable stew, and Malabar chicken biryani.

## Practical Logistics & Seasonality
- **Best Travel Window:** September to March provides mild, pleasant weather. June to August (Monsoon season) is the traditional peak period for restorative Ayurvedic treatments.
- **Transit:** Kochi International Airport (COK) serves as the primary gateway, operating completely on solar power.
